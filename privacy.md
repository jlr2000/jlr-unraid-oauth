# Privacy Policy

**Effective date: October 10, 2026**

JLR Unraid Integration provides personal Google Drive integration for its authorized account owner.

## Data access and use

The application accesses Google Drive files and folders as authorized by the user. Permission may cover more files than those used in a particular operation. Access may include reading, transferring, organizing, and deleting files as needed for user-configured operations.

## Storage and sharing

Authentication tokens are stored privately. Data is retained under the account owner's control and used solely for authorized operations. It is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties.

## User control

The account owner controls retained information and may revoke Google access at any time through [Google Account settings](https://myaccount.google.com/connections).

## Contact

Questions may be directed through the [public project repository](https://github.com/jlr2000/jlr-unraid-oauth).
